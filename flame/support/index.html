Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a subscription? +
Add a subscription from the main screen by entering the service name, amount, billing cycle, and next renewal date, then save it to your list.
How do reminders work? +
Flame sends reminder notifications before an upcoming renewal so you have time to review, cancel, or budget for the charge.
Can I cancel subscriptions through the app? +
No. Flame helps you monitor subscriptions and renewal dates, but cancellations must be completed directly with the subscription provider.
Is my data secure? +
Flame is designed to keep your subscription data private and only uses the information needed to provide tracking and reminder features.
I found a bug — what should I do? +
Email support@d6t.in with a short description of the issue, your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce it.

Troubleshooting

  • App not sending reminders: check notification permissions in iPhone Settings.
  • Missing subscriptions: make sure the subscription was saved successfully.
  • Login issues: try signing in again with Apple Sign-In.
