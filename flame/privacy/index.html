Flame Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Flame handles information related to subscription tracking and support requests.

Overview

Flame is a subscription tracking app designed to help you monitor recurring payments and reminders. We only collect and use information necessary to operate the app and provide support.

Information We May Collect

  • Subscription details you enter, such as service name, amount, and billing dates.
  • Basic account information required for sign-in or syncing, if applicable.
  • Support details you send when contacting us, such as your email address and issue description.

How We Use Information

  • To show your subscriptions and renewal schedule inside the app.
  • To send reminders related to upcoming subscription renewals.
  • To respond to support requests and improve app reliability.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is only shared when necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security and integrity of Flame.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and limit access to the data needed to operate Flame. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to keep your data protected.

Your Choices

You can update or remove subscription information within the app. If you have privacy questions or want to request deletion of support communications, contact us at support@d6t.in.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email support@d6t.in.
